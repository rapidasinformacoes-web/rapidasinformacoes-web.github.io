Privacy Policy
App name: SupoClip (personal use) Last updated: October 30, 2026 Contact: rapidasinformacoes@gmail.com

1. Who we are
SupoClip is a personal, self-hosted tool operated by Gustavo Rezende Oliveira ("we", "the operator"). It is used by its operator to create short video clips and publish them to the operator's own social media accounts.

2. Information we access
When you connect your TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, we receive only the information you authorize:

Basic profile information (display name and avatar) through the user.info.basic scope.
The list of videos you have posted through the app, through the video.list scope.
Permission to upload and publish videos you choose to post, through the video.publish scope.
We also store an access token and a refresh token so the app can publish on your behalf.

3. How we use information
We use this information only to:

Show which account is connected.
Publish or schedule videos that you explicitly choose to post.
Display basic performance data (such as views and likes) of videos posted through the app.
We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or any unrelated purpose.

4. Storage and security
Tokens and app data are stored on the operator's own computer or server, in an encrypted form where applicable. We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties, except where required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion
Data is kept only while your account is connected. You can disconnect your TikTok account in the app at any time, which deletes the stored tokens. You can also revoke access in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and websites. To request deletion of any other data, contact us at the email above and we will respond within 30 days.

6. Children
This app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

7. Changes
We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows the latest version.
